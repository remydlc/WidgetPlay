Widget Play

Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

SupportPrivacy

The short version: We never collect, receive, or share your personal data. Everything you add to Widget Play stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Widget Play is published by nine3one2.com ("we"). The app stores the following only on your iPhone, in storage shared between the app and its widgets. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Photos you choose, and background-removed versions of them
  • Startup sounds you import
  • Maintenance reminders, countdowns, notes, and widget designs
  • Your settings, such as theme, fonts, and colors

Photos

When you add a photo, you choose it with the iOS photo picker. Widget Play receives only the photos you select, not your whole library. Background removal runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device Vision framework. Photos are never uploaded.

Sounds and Shortcuts

Sounds you import through the Files picker are copied into the app on your device. The startup-sound feature runs through a Shortcuts automation you set up yourself; it runs locally on your iPhone.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No location access
  • No network requests, other than the App Store checking your purchase

Deleting your data

Because everything is on your device, you control it. You can remove any photo, sound, reminder, countdown, or design in the app. Deleting Widget Play from your iPhone removes all of its data.

Children

Widget Play does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above. If the app ever begins collecting data, we'll say so here and in the App Store listing before it does.
