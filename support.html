Requirements

CarPlay widgets require iOS 26 or later and a CarPlay-compatible car. Widgets also work on your Home Screen, Lock Screen, and in StandBy.

Common questions

How do I add a widget to CarPlay?
  1. Connect your iPhone to CarPlay at least once so your car appears in Settings.
  2. On your iPhone, open Settings › General › CarPlay and choose your car.
  3. Tap Widgets and add a Widget Play widget.

iOS requires you to add widgets yourself; no app can place them for you. After that, changes you make in the app update the widget in your car automatically.

How do I choose which design a widget shows?

Add the My Designs widget, then touch and hold it and choose Edit Widget to pick a design. Add it more than once to show different designs.

How do I set up a startup sound?

iOS doesn't let apps run on their own when CarPlay connects, so every startup-sound app uses a one-time Shortcuts automation:

  1. Open Shortcuts and go to the Automation tab.
  2. Tap +, choose CarPlay, select Connects, and pick Run Immediately.
  3. Add the Play Car Sound action from Widget Play.

The app walks you through this under Startup Sound.

My widget isn't showing my latest changes

iOS decides when widgets refresh. Open Widget Play and change any setting to prompt an update. In CarPlay, reconnecting helps; if a widget still looks old, remove it from the CarPlay widget list and add it again.

Why does the widget look different in CarPlay?

CarPlay shows widgets at the small size and handles backgrounds differently than your Home Screen. Widget Play adapts automatically so your photos and theme still appear. In CarPlay, widgets are for viewing only; tapping them doesn't open the app.

Background removal says it couldn't find a subject

It works best when your car (or person, or pet) stands out clearly from what's behind it. Try a photo with more contrast between the subject and the background. Your original photo is always kept.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap Restore Purchase on the main screen or in the unlock screen, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Family Sharing members get the unlock automatically.

Is it a subscription?

No. The unlock is a one-time purchase. You pay once and keep it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Your photos and designs stay saved; premium items are locked until you unlock again.

Does Widget Play collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
